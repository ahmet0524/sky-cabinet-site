Questions

Straight answers

Is the in-home measurement really free?

Yes. We measure, draw your room and give you a written quote with no obligation.

How long does a kitchen take?

It depends on the cabinet line you choose. In-stock cabinets arrive faster than made-to-order lines. Your written quote includes an exact delivery date.

Can I buy cabinets and install them myself?

Yes. You can buy cabinets only, with pickup at our Norfolk showroom or delivery to your home.

How do the free door samples work?

Borrow up to 3 full-size doors for 7 days. We take a $25 refundable deposit per door and return it when you bring the doors back.

Who installs the cabinets?

Installation is done by our Virginia-licensed installation partner and comes with our 1-year installation warranty.
