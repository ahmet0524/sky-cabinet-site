Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

KLF Trading LLC, doing business as SKY Cabinet ("we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through skycabinetva.com, phone calls, text messages and email, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email and address or ZIP code.
  • Project details: measurements, photos and videos of your space, style preferences and budget.
  • Records of calls and messages with our team.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare estimates.
  • To schedule measurements, deliveries and installations.
  • To send project updates and answer your questions.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you text us or check the SMS consent box on our contact form, we will text you only about your inquiry, estimates, appointments and project updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our phone, email, website and file storage providers, or when required by law.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Project photos and files are stored in secure cloud accounts that only our team can access.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
