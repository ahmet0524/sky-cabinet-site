## What a coastal kitchen needs

Virginia Beach homes live close to the water, and kitchens here deal with humid summers and salt air. A few things are worth thinking about before you choose cabinets:

- **Ventilation matters.** A range hood that vents outside and a bathroom fan that actually runs keep moisture away from cabinet boxes and doors. Plan the hood with the cabinets, not after.
- **Ask how the cabinet boxes are built.** Before you buy anywhere, ask what the boxes, shelves and back panels are made of, and how the finish is applied. Cabinets near the sink and dishwasher see the most moisture.
- **Leave room for air.** Cabinets set tight against an exterior wall or over a sink base with a slow leak are where problems start. A careful installer checks for leaks and levels everything before the cabinets go in.
- **Pick colors for coastal light.** Bright coastal light makes colors look lighter and cooler than they do in a store. ## How to compare kitchen cabinet companies in Virginia Beach

Whoever you choose, ask every company the same questions:

- **Is the measurement free, and is the quote written and itemized?** A quote that lists every cabinet, the hardware and the installation is easy to compare. A single total is not.
- **Who installs, and are they licensed in Virginia?** Ask for the installation warranty in writing.
- **What is included, and what is extra?** Removal of old cabinets, haul-away and countertop templating are often where prices differ.
- **Can I see the doors at home?** Colors look different under your own lights and next to your own floors.
- **When will the cabinets arrive?** In-stock cabinets arrive faster than made-to-order lines. A good quote gives you a delivery date before the old kitchen comes out.
